# How to Calculate Share of Synthesis

Share of Synthesis (SoS) is one of the most critical metrics in AI SEO. While traditional Share of Voice measures how often your brand is mentioned, **Share of Synthesis measures how often your content is selected as a foundational source** when AI search engines construct an answer.

Understanding and calculating your Share of Synthesis allows you to measure true information influence, track content performance over time, and compare your citation share directly against competitors.

---

## What Is Share of Synthesis (SoS)?

In AI-powered search (such as ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews), answers are generated through a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline. 

When a user submits a prompt:
1. The AI system searches the web for relevant candidate documents.
2. It evaluates and selects a subset of those documents to synthesize an answer.
3. It attaches citations to the selected sources.

**Share of Synthesis represents the percentage of total synthesized responses where your website's URL or domain is cited as an underlying source.**

---

## The Share of Synthesis Formula

To calculate Share of Synthesis across a target prompt set:

```latex
Share of Synthesis (SoS) = ( Number of Synthesized Answers Citing Your Domain / Total Number of Synthesized Answers Evaluated ) × 100
```

### Example Calculation:
Suppose you track a prompt set of **50 target prompts** across 4 major AI platforms (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude):
- Total answer evaluations = 50 prompts × 4 platforms = 200 responses
- Your domain is cited as a source in **48** of those responses.

```latex
SoS = ( 48 / 200 ) × 100 = 24%
```

Your brand commands a **24% Share of Synthesis** across that prompt universe.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide to Measuring SoS

### Step 1: Define Your Target Prompt Universe
Select 20 to 100 high-intent questions that represent your category, products, use cases, and buyer decision stages. Avoid single keywords—use full conversational questions.

### Step 2: Establish Minimum Sample Size ($n \ge 7$)
Because AI answers are probabilistic, running a prompt once is insufficient. To reduce standard error below 0.10, run each prompt at least **7 times** (or use rolling 14-day data).

### Step 3: Audit Source Citations
For each generated response, record:
- Is your domain cited? (Binary: Yes / No)
- Which specific URL was cited?
- Which competitors were cited?
- Was your brand mentioned without a citation?

### Step 4: Compute Overall & Sub-Segment SoS
Calculate your aggregate SoS score, then segment it by platform (e.g., ChatGPT SoS vs. Perplexity SoS) and intent stage (Awareness vs. Evaluation).

---

## Interpreting Your SoS Score

| SoS Range | Benchmark Status | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| **0% – 10%** | **Incalculable / Low Visibility** | Urgent content restructuring; add BLUF summaries and comparison tables. |
| **11% – 25%** | **Developing Presence** | Focus on topic authority gaps and entity consistency. |
| **26% – 45%** | **Strong Category Citation** | Maintain coverage; monitor competitor movement. |
| **46%+** | **Dominant Synthesis Share** | High authority; protect source footprint across platforms. |

---

## How to Improve Your Share of Synthesis

If your Share of Synthesis is lower than desired, focus on these three high-impact optimizations:

1. **Place Answers in the First 100 Words (BLUF):** Over 44% of citations are drawn from the first 30% of content.
2. **Convert Prose into Comparative Tables:** Machine-readable tables enjoy a 2.5x citation advantage over paragraph text.
3. **Build Entity & Fact Density:** Ensure key specifications, statistics, and definitions are explicitly stated and easily extractable.

---

## Summary

Share of Synthesis replaces vague ranking metrics with a clear, empirical measurement of how deeply your content powers AI answers. By tracking SoS monthly across fixed prompt sets, your team can measure the exact impact of your AI SEO optimizations.

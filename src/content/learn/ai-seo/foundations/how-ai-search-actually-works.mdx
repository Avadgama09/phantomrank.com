Traditional search engines like Google ask: *"Which indexed URL best matches these keywords?"* AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini ask a fundamentally different question: *"What is the most accurate, concise, and cited answer I can synthesize for this user prompt?"*

Understanding this distinction is the cornerstone of modern search intelligence and AI SEO.
